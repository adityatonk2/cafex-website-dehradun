Our story

A coffee truck,
a few tables,
and time to sit.

CAFEX serves coffee on Rajpur Road, Dehradun. Order at the window, then settle in outdoors under the trees with a cappuccino, a shake or a stack of pancakes.

Guests describe the place as cosy and relaxed, with friendly staff and quick service. Come alone, with friends, or on a date.
